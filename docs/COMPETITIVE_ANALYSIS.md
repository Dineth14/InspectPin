# Comparing field inspection workflows

| Workflow | Documented capability | What to demonstrate with InspectPin |
|---|---|---|
| RealWear My Camera | RealWear documents a wearable camera application with photo and video capture and voice interaction. Hands-free capture is an established category. [Manufacturer camera guide](https://support.realwear.com/knowledge/my-camera-overview) | Capture an engineering nameplate, ask a question about that specific image, hear the answer and retrieve the original from the inspection. |
| SafetyCulture visual inspection | The published sample report shows a structured inspection, recorded observations and photographic evidence. [Manufacturer sample report](https://wp-website.safetyculture.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2025/07/Visual-Inspection-Checklist-Sample-Report.pdf) | Open a device-originated inspection, compare the answer with the original, add an inspector note and export the report. |
| Technician's existing phone | A practical evaluation baseline is photographing a label, opening it, entering the observation and attaching it to a report. The time and effort vary with the phone and software. | Ask the same technician to perform the same task twice in different orders. Record elapsed time, transcription corrections, missed fields and handling interruptions. |

InspectPin's proposed advantage for small maintenance teams is the combination of two physical controls, retained SD originals, spoken image-grounded questions, provider choice and a reviewable inspection record in one workflow. A field trial must establish the size of any benefit. Current evidence supports a demonstrated prototype; claims about comparative accuracy, ruggedness, adoption, cost savings or a unique market position would require additional data.
