# Evaluation rehearsal, 26 November 2026

Six groups share an hour. Arrive 30 minutes early with the charged unit, tested SD card, hotspot, a printed de-energized label and the receiver/app already checked. Record the release, account/device ID, time and network mode on the rehearsal sheet. Use a label whose voltage, model and units can be checked by a second person.

| Time | Demonstrator action | What the assessor can verify |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:40 | Show the wearable and describe a technician trying to document a nameplate while handling tools. | The product addresses a concrete field workflow. |
| 0:40–1:15 | Power on using the battery. Show a fresh heartbeat and identify whether the receiver runs on the laptop or a verified public host. | Real hardware is connected in the stated mode. |
| 1:15–2:30 | Press PHOTO. Open its new inspection. Press ASK and ask for the voltage followed by the model number. | Compare each spoken field with the visible label. Explain an unreadable field honestly. |
| 2:30–3:45 | Show the original, save a human observation and open the exported PDF. | The evidence, timestamp and inspector input are traceable. |
| 3:45–4:45 | On a rehearsed recording build, make a short note, replay it, then open a previously captured silent clip. Label earlier evidence with its time. | Actual recording and retrieval, separate from inference. |
| 4:45–5:30 | Show the architecture and AI configuration. Describe the hosted qualification gate and the proposed pilot. | Engineering choices, constraints and commercial direction. |

For a two-minute slot show battery, fresh PHOTO, one spoken answer, original and PDF. For unreliable Internet use the prepared LAN receiver and local Ollama models. If a current run fails, display the diagnostic and clearly identify prior physical evidence. With consent, film a real successful rehearsal showing the board, label, speaker and app together. No backup video of this new release is asserted here.

Rubric evidence: functionality comes from the physical cycle and actual result; differentiation from completing one inspection without handling a phone at each step; appearance and documentation from the assembled enclosure, app and report; presentation from one timed scenario and a concise explanation of limitations. Record the observed outcome rather than assigning a predicted score.

For a strict five-minute slot, end the live sequence at 4:45 with the evidence and one sentence about the pilot. For an extended 8–10 minute rehearsal, demonstrate manual audio stop, silent video seek, the original download, follow-up question, provider settings and a timed offline retry. Ask a second person to time the sequence and compare the spoken number and units with the physical label. A backup film requires the operator's consent and a camera showing the board, label, speaker and app. Label it with capture date, build, network mode and any cuts.
